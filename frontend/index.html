3 Key Findings from Historical Data Analysis (Assignment Unit Economics)

Based strictly on analyzing our ~2,000 historical orders and applying the official take-home assignment cost benchmarks.

1. Stopping Serial Returners (2+ Past Returns)

Historical data shows customers with 2 or more past returns (RTOs) fail delivery 76.5% of the time. Halting these specific repeat returners saves ₹169.40 per order in wasted courier (forward ₹65 + return ₹65) and packaging fees without hurting genuine buyers.

2. Protecting First-Time Buyers (₹300 Ad Spend)

Marketing spends ₹300 in Meta ads (CAC) to acquire each new customer. If we cancel a first-time buyer, we lose that ₹300 plus ₹402 gross margin. Our engine never auto-cancels first orders; instead, it sends a WhatsApp message or offers ₹40 off to pay online.

3. Pincode Fairness (No Unfair Blacklisting)

Over 82% of pincodes only have 1 or 2 past orders. If an area had just 1 order that happened to return, blacklisting it would choke off future sales. The engine requires at least 5 orders before using an area's return rate as a negative factor.
